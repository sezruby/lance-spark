# KNN perf — June 2026 (cloud, |R|=100M)

Cloud-storage Lance dataset on Azure ABFSS (same-region storage), Spark 3.5.4
standalone, 8 executors × 4 cores, 16 GiB executor memory.
Bench: `LanceKnnSingleVsSparkBenchmark` against the `huge` scale (|R|=100M,
dim=128, IVF-PQ, |L|=5000, k=10, nprobes=16, refineFactor=8).

## Headline

| Config | Median wall-clock | vs. SINGLE | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SPARK_DIST** (8 exec × 4 cores) | **42 s** | **~13×** | mapPartitions over `LanceProbe.probe`, P=24/32 |
| SPARK_DIST (4 exec × 4 cores) | 77 s | ~6× | linear scaling vs. 8 exec |
| TRIVIAL2 (1 exec, `LanceProbe.probe` loop) | 433 s | 1.13× | floor for "shipped to one executor" |
| **SPARK** (`kNearestJoin`, probeParallelism=1) | **472 s** | 1.04× | 3-stage pipeline overhead at L=5000 ≈ break-even |
| TRIVIAL (1 exec, `Dataset.newScan` loop) | 473 s | 1.04× | identical to SPARK |
| **SINGLE** (driver-side `Dataset.newScan` loop) | **490 s** | 1.00× | baseline |
| TRIVIAL3 (probe + materialize) | 540 s | 1.10× | extra materialize cost |

(SPARK_DIST = dist-only 5 reps, range 41.2–49.6 s. 4-exec data point is a
separate dist-only run at the same scale.)

## What this says

- **The naive distributed mapPartitions loop is the right shape at this scale**, not
  the staged `kNearestJoin` pipeline. Its 3-stage shuffle / merge / materialize
  imposes a structural ~3-4 s overhead per query batch that breaks even with
  driver-side single-machine until L is much larger.
- TRIVIAL ≈ TRIVIAL2 ≈ SINGLE (within 4 %) confirms that **single-task Spark adds
  near-zero overhead** vs. the driver loop — the win comes only from spreading
  work across executors.
- Linear scaling 4 → 8 executors (77 s → 42 s) shows the workload is
  parallelism-bound, not I/O-bound, at this configuration.
- TRIVIAL3's extra materialize step (probe → take rid column) costs ~10 % vs.
  TRIVIAL2 — useful baseline for evaluating future projection-pushdown work.

## Caveats

- All numbers are **wall-clock medians of 3-5 timed reps**, with one warmup discarded.
- `kNearestJoin`'s `probeParallelism > 1` is **anti-optimization** at this L
  (3-7× slower than `probeParallelism=1` everywhere we tested) — left for a
  future fix. See [`IMPL_PLAN.md`](IMPL_PLAN.md) for the fragment-grouping notes.
- Long bench runs (3+ hours) reproducibly hit transient executor losses from
  Lance ↔ ABFSS network flakes. Mitigated for measurement by running SPARK_DIST
  reps in their own ~5-minute submission with `BENCH_CONFIGS=SPARK_DIST`.

## Reproducing

```sh
# Build the benchmark fat jar
./mvnw -pl lance-spark-knn_2.12 -am package -Pbenchmark -DskipTests

# Submit with your cluster's submit tool. Required env on driver+executors:
#   AZURE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT_NAME, AZURE_STORAGE_ACCOUNT_KEY (for Lance abfss reads)
#   BENCH_DATA_PATH=abfss://CONTAINER@ACCOUNT.dfs.core.windows.net/PREFIX
#   BENCH_SCALES=huge
#   BENCH_NUM_L=5000
#   BENCH_REPEATS=5
#   BENCH_REUSE_DATA=true     (skip dataset rebuild)
#   BENCH_CONFIGS=SPARK_DIST  (skip the 2-hour SINGLE/SPARK/TRIVIAL preamble)
#
# And these Spark conf overrides for cluster stability:
#   spark.network.timeout=120s
#   spark.task.maxFailures=4
```

`BENCH_CONFIGS` accepts a comma-separated subset of
`SINGLE,SPARK,TRIVIAL,TRIVIAL2,TRIVIAL3,SPARK_DIST` so you can isolate one
configuration without paying the full bench wall-clock.

## Operational notes

| Symptom | Frequency | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Single executor disassociates mid-run | 4 of 4 long jobs (3+ h) hit ≥ 1 | `spark.task.maxFailures=4` reschedules on a fresh pod (~5 s) |
| 60-min hang per failure | seen with `task.maxFailures=1` (default) | `spark.task.maxFailures=4` + lower `spark.network.timeout` |
| Lance retry budget compounds (~3-9 min) | always present on a pod-network glitch | `OBJECT_STORE_CLIENT_RETRY_TIMEOUT=30` shortens per-attempt window |

The single-executor hang we observed has the same surface symptom across all
four incidents we have logs for: `error sending request` (transport-level, not
HTTP status) inside Lance's `MicrosoftAzureBuilder` GET on `auxiliary.idx`.
Per-pod, not per-account (other executors don't show retries). Most likely a
connection-pool / pod-network state issue inside the long-lived JVM, not
Lance-side or storage-side. Cluster-side investigation is in flight.
